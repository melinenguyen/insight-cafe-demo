3Setup · teach it the brand

The café learns the brand before it opens.

One form, fifteen short sections, filled once. Everything the crew needs to think like the brand’s own team. Nothing required is left blank: the café won’t open until it is.

  • The brand: promise, voice, look, words it never uses
  • Products, prices and offer rules
  • Who buys: three personas
  • Competitors: who to learn from, who to watch
  • The ad library: the angles and concepts we test
  • Sources, the calendar and the occasions that matter
  • The team, their lanes, the report times

5Setup · build the office

One folder, two repos and a clock. Nobody’s laptop stays on.

Claude Code turns the profile into the café’s folder, then it moves into two GitHub repos: a private brain (code and memory) and a public site (the office, locked with a passphrase).
